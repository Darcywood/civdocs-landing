# Competitor Research: CivDocs vs Simpro (simPRO)

> **Purpose:** Source material for `/compare/civdocs-vs-simpro`. Research only. Frame: honest/balanced, slight CivDocs lean. **The single most important, well-evidenced wedge: Simpro was built for SERVICE TRADES (electrical, plumbing, HVAC, security, fire) — NOT civil construction.** This isn't spin — it shows up repeatedly, including in a Capterra review from a Varicon customer: *"We are a civil construction business, and Simpro was in effect made for electricians and plumbers."* CivDocs is purpose-built for civil/earthworks/plant hire. That mismatch is the whole story, backed up by Simpro's pricing/contract reputation.

---

## 1. Company snapshot

- **Product:** Simpro (formerly simPRO) — "AI-first operating platform for residential and commercial trades." Field service management (FSM) + job management.
- **Origin:** Founded **2002, Brisbane, Australia** — by an electrical business owner + a software engineering student. So: Australian, but **electrical/trades DNA from day one.**
- **Scale:** 250,000+ users globally, 9,000+ businesses. Offices in AU/NZ, US, UK/Ireland. Part of **Simpro Group** (also owns BigChange, **AroFlo**, ClockShark — it acquired competitor AroFlo).
- **Positioning:** "Double trades business profitability." Heavy current AI push (Simpro Lightning, "Cooper" AI assistant, JustAsk, FieldReady, GPS time tracking).
- **Category:** FSM / job management for **service & maintenance trades** with project capability bolted on.

## 2. What Simpro does (feature inventory)

- **Quoting / estimating** — digital takeoff templates, fast quotes split into cost centres, quote→job conversion. A flagship strength (reviewers love quoting speed).
- **Scheduling & dispatching** — assign techs, track progress, real-time updates. Strong.
- **Job management** — service jobs + multi-stage projects, recurring/maintenance jobs, progress billing.
- **Inventory / stock / warehouse management** — strong; catalogue + supplier pricing. (Very trades-oriented — parts/materials for service work.)
- **Asset management** — asset/job history (a review flags weakness here for multi-part assets).
- **Invoicing / payments / cash flow** — quote-to-cash, integrated payments.
- **Job costing** — real-time job costing, cost-centre breakdowns.
- **Timesheets / GPS time tracking** — AI-prompted clock in/out verified against location.
- **Digital Forms** (add-on) — conditional-logic forms, gov-compliant forms.
- **Compliance / safety** — integrated.
- **AI (Simpro Lightning):** Cooper assistant, JustAsk (NL reporting), FieldReady (tech training), JobReady/JobBrief (pre-job briefs), Two-Way SMS messaging.
- **Simtrac** (add-on) — fleet GPS tracking (hardware trackers).
- **Data Feed, Maintenance Planner, Takeoffs** — paid add-ons.

**Integrations:** 100+ — Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks, Sage, NetSuite, suppliers, etc. Broad.

## 3. ICP — who Simpro is really for

- **Service & maintenance trades:** electrical, plumbing, HVAC, security, fire protection, roofing, solar, facilities, AV, low-voltage. Stated explicitly across their site and review profiles.
- Growing-to-large trade businesses with **recurring service work, dispatch, parts/inventory, and quoting** as core daily workflows.
- Residential + commercial trades; maintenance teams; some larger project work.
- **NOT positioned for civil/earthworks.** No civil-specific workflows (plant hire logbooks, machine-hour costing, civil job costing, MGA/survey, earthmoving dockets).

**Overlap with CivDocs:** Both are AU-founded, both do timesheets/invoicing/job costing/scheduling. But Simpro is **service-trade-shaped** (dispatch, inventory, recurring service jobs, quoting) while CivDocs is **civil/plant-hire-shaped** (plant logbooks, machine-hour costing, pre-starts, earthworks job costing). The workflows diverge sharply under the hood.

## 4. Pricing — secondary wedge (and a real reputational soft spot)

- **No public pricing.** Custom quote; tailored to team size (office + field) + add-ons. Monthly or annual billing. No free version, no free trial.
- **Contract lock-in reputation — well-evidenced and damaging.** A Capterra reviewer described being offered **"a lock-in contract of 3-years or 5-years at an 'expected' 8% per annum (CPI + 5%) locked in to a minimum user quantity,"** with the alternative being a *"12% (subject to change) per annum increase,"* calling it an *"extortionate approach"* and noting Simpro "bought out key competitor AroFlo and now playing this monopolistic card."
- Another reviewer: tried to exit after both sides agreed it wasn't working, but were held to a *"binding contract"* after a sales VP reneged on a parting agreement.
- Add-ons (Digital Forms, Data Feeds, Simtrac, Takeoffs) cost extra — modular pricing stacks up.

**Angle for CivDocs:** **Free trial + transparent tiers + no multi-year lock-in** vs Simpro's quote-only, no-trial, multi-year contracts with annual escalators and minimum-user commitments. Strong, concrete, review-sourced wedge — but cite as user experiences, not blanket fact.

## 5. Strengths (be honest)

- **Mature, deep, 20+ year platform** — comprehensive FSM. For a service trade, genuinely excellent.
- **Best-in-class quoting/estimating** — repeatedly praised; quote→job→invoice flow is fast and strong.
- **Strong inventory/stock management** — important for trades, a real differentiator.
- **Scheduling & dispatch** — robust.
- **Big integration ecosystem** (100+), incl. Xero + MYOB.
- **Serious AI investment** (Simpro Lightning / Cooper) — ahead on AI features.
- **Established, global, well-supported** (24/6 support across regions, local AU/NZ implementation teams).
- Good overall satisfaction (~82% / 4.3–ish across review sites).

## 6. Weaknesses / gaps (where CivDocs can win)

- **Built for trades, not civil.** THE core point. No civil/plant-hire-specific workflows. Civil customers explicitly say it's "made for electricians and plumbers."
- **Steep learning curve / complexity** — recurring theme: "clunky and unintuitive," "multiple clicks to get to common tasks," "requires a full-time inputter," "extremely time consuming," significant training needed.
- **Contract lock-in + price escalation reputation** (see §4) — multi-year, minimum users, CPI+5%/8–12% annual rises, hard to exit. Genuinely damaging in reviews.
- **No free trial, no self-serve, quote-only.**
- **Report customisation limited** without advanced skills/external tools.
- **Mobile app limitations** — doesn't fully mirror desktop; asset management weak for multi-part assets.
- **Add-on pricing** — core features fine, but civil-relevant extras (forms, data feeds) cost more.
- **Overkill for a small civil operator** whose needs are timesheets/pre-starts/plant logbooks/invoicing, not dispatch + inventory + recurring service.

## 7. CivDocs differentiation angles (for the page)

1. **Civil-native vs trades-native.** The headline. Simpro is built for service trades (electrical/plumbing/HVAC). CivDocs is built for civil contractors, earthworks, and plant hire — the workflows, terminology, and costing are different. Lead with this.
2. **Plant hire logbooks, machine-hour costing, civil job costing, pre-starts, earthmoving dockets** — first-class in CivDocs; absent/awkward in Simpro.
3. **Free trial + transparent tiers vs multi-year lock-in contracts** with annual escalators and minimum-user commitments. (Cite review experiences.)
4. **Simplicity vs complexity.** CivDocs is lighter and faster to adopt; Simpro is documented as complex/training-heavy ("needs a full-time inputter").
5. **No dispatch/inventory bloat.** Civil contractors don't need Simpro's service-dispatch + warehouse inventory machinery; CivDocs gives the civil-relevant tools without the trades overhead.
6. **Founder-built for civil, ships fast** — speaks the civil operator's language.

**Keep it fair:** Simpro is a strong, mature product — for trades. Don't claim it's bad software; claim it's the wrong shape for civil. Acknowledge its quoting/inventory/AI strengths. The honest frame: *"Simpro is a powerful, mature platform — but it was built for service trades like electrical, plumbing and HVAC, with dispatch, inventory and recurring-service workflows at its core. CivDocs is built specifically for civil contractors and plant hire businesses — plant logbooks, machine-hour costing, pre-starts and civil job costing — with a free trial, transparent pricing, and no multi-year lock-in."*
