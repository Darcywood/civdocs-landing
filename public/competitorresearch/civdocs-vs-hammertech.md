# Competitor Research: CivDocs vs HammerTech

> **Purpose:** Source material for `/compare/civdocs-vs-hammertech`. Research only. Frame: honest/balanced, slight CivDocs lean. **Key insight: this is a category mismatch, and that's the whole story.** HammerTech is a **safety/compliance platform for large GCs and enterprise builders**, not an all-in-one operational tool for small civil contractors. The page should help a searcher realise they may be comparing two different kinds of product — and that CivDocs fits the small-civil-contractor use case HammerTech wasn't built for.

---

## 1. Company snapshot

- **Product:** HammerTech — construction **safety, compliance & site-operations** platform. "Built by safety professionals for the realities of the jobsite."
- **Origin:** Australian-owned, developers based in Australia (per reviews praising local ownership). Now strongly global.
- **Scale:** 400+ contractors, **2 million+ workers enrolled**, 100,000+ daily sign-ins across UK, Ireland, Australia, NZ, Canada, US, supporting ~$100B AUD annual construction volume. Used by major builders: DPR, Shawmut, Icon, Kapitol, Gray, Ryan Companies, Holder.
- **Category:** EHS / construction safety management. NOT a job-costing/invoicing/timesheet operational tool.

## 2. What HammerTech does (feature inventory)

All safety/compliance-centric:
- **Worker onboarding & site inductions / orientations** (incl. site-specific video orientations).
- **Subcontractor & crew compliance management** — the entire subcontractor lifecycle is a standout strength.
- **Pre-task planning** — JHAs, PTPs, RAMS, daily briefings.
- **Site inspections & safety observations.**
- **High-risk permitting** — confined space, hot work, work at heights, etc.
- **Incident & near-miss reporting.**
- **Document, license & credential tracking** (SDSs, safety plans).
- **Equipment / asset management** (safety-oriented).
- **HammerTech Insights** — real-time safety reporting, 30+ out-of-box reports, leading/lagging indicators.
- **HammerTech Intelligence / AI** — AI-powered risk identification ("Master Construction Safety with AI").
- **HammerTechGO** — pre-configured fast-start package: 25 pre-built forms + 100+ checklists, 2-week go-live, for mid-market GCs / teams new to safety platforms.

**Integrations:** Integrates with Procore (Gray Construction case study pairs HammerTech + Procore). Connects into existing construction tech stacks. **Notably weak on:** document management integration and broader business systems — a reviewer flagged "lack of integration with document management and other software; its key focus is compliance and safety."

## 3. ICP — who HammerTech is really for

- **General contractors, EPCs, and owners** managing **multiple projects and large, distributed workforces** with heavy subcontractor coordination.
- Enterprise + mid-market builders who need to standardise safety across many sites and many subcontractors.
- Self-perform GCs.
- **The unlimited-user model is built around getting every subcontractor and worker onto the GC's safety platform** — this only makes sense at GC scale.

**Overlap with CivDocs:** Minimal in function. A small civil contractor might use HammerTech *as a subcontractor* because a GC mandated it (it's free for subs — the GC pays). But HammerTech does **not** do CivDocs' core jobs: timesheets→payroll, invoicing, job costing, plant hire logbooks, machine-hour costing, Xero/MYOB sync. Different product category.

## 4. Pricing

- **No public pricing.** Custom, **sliding scale based on annual construction revenue + number of active job sites.**
- **Unlimited users** — flat-fee model, no per-seat. Subcontractors are **free** (GC holds the license).
- Pricing includes implementation, onboarding, training, support, integrations, enterprise security.
- HammerTechGO positioned as the cheaper/faster entry; pitched against the $5k–$20k cost of consultant-built paper systems.

**Angle for CivDocs:** HammerTech's pricing is scaled to enterprise construction *revenue* and sold to GCs — a small civil contractor isn't the buyer. CivDocs is priced (transparent tiers) and built for the small civil/plant-hire operator directly.

## 5. Strengths (be honest)

- **Best-in-class construction safety & compliance.** Deep, mature, purpose-built. If safety/compliance at scale is the need, HammerTech is genuinely excellent.
- **Subcontractor lifecycle management** is a real differentiator.
- **Unlimited-user / free-for-subs model** drives adoption — smart for safety culture across a big site.
- **Enterprise credibility & scale** (2M+ workers, marquee builders, OSHA VPP praise).
- **Australian-owned, responsive ownership** (reviewers praise local owners listening to feedback).
- HammerTechGO lowers the barrier for smaller/newer safety programs.
- Strong G2/review scores on customisability and support.

## 6. Weaknesses / gaps (relative to CivDocs' use case)

- **Not an all-in-one ops/finance tool.** No timesheets→payroll, no invoicing, no job costing, no plant hire logbooks, no Xero/MYOB accounting sync. This is the core point.
- **Learning curve / complexity.** Repeated review theme: "significant learning curve," "overwhelming/cumbersome for new users," one reviewer wanted "a more elementary version… something even the Vietnam Vets can use on their Nokias."
- **Inspection module slow** (multiple reviews: "extremely slow," "cumbersome").
- **Limited analytics customisation** — heavy reporting needs pushed to Power BI.
- **Weak document-management integration** (reviewer-flagged).
- **Daily-changing QR sign-in codes** can't be printed/left on site (workflow gripe).
- **Built for GC scale** — overkill and wrong-shaped for a small civil contractor running their own crews/plant.

## 7. CivDocs differentiation angles (for the page)

The page's job is mostly **clarifying the category difference honestly**, then showing CivDocs fits the searcher's likely need:
1. **Different categories.** HammerTech = enterprise safety/compliance for GCs. CivDocs = all-in-one operations for small-to-mid civil contractors & plant hire. State this plainly and fairly.
2. **CivDocs runs the business; HammerTech runs the safety program.** Timesheets, invoicing, job costing, plant logbooks, machine-hour costing, accounting sync — none of which HammerTech does.
3. **Right-sized & affordable for small civil.** HammerTech's buyer is a GC pricing on construction revenue. CivDocs is built and priced for the operator.
4. **CivDocs still covers site forms / pre-starts / SWMS** — so a small contractor gets the compliance basics *plus* the operational tooling, in one place, without an enterprise EHS platform.
5. **Simplicity** — CivDocs is designed to be picked up fast vs HammerTech's documented learning curve.

**Keep it fair:** Do NOT imply CivDocs replaces HammerTech for a large GC running enterprise safety across 100 sites — it doesn't, and shouldn't claim to. The honest frame: *"If you're a GC standardising safety across many subcontractors, HammerTech is purpose-built for that. If you're a civil contractor or plant hire business trying to run timesheets, invoicing, job costing and plant logbooks — plus your pre-starts and SWMS — in one place, that's what CivDocs is for."*
